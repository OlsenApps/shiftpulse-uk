ShiftPulse UK – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Developer: OlsenApps (Marcin Lewandowski), United Kingdom.
Contact: olsenapps@proton.me

Summary

ShiftPulse UK works entirely on your phone. It has no accounts, no developer server, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. The developer does not receive, collect or have access to any of your data.

Data stored on your device

The following information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your answers in the setup wizard (pay rates, contracted hours, pay week, overtime, allowances, tax code, NI letter, pension and student loan settings, year-to-date totals you enter);
  • your shifts, clock-in and clock-out times, break times, leave days and notes;
  • identifiers of NFC tags you register for clocking in;
  • app settings such as language, colours, app lock and notification preferences;
  • the optional name you enter, shown at the top of the app.

Pay, tax and National Insurance figures are calculated on your phone.

Notifications, NFC and app lock

While a shift is running the app shows a notification with the shift timer and estimated earnings; you can hide the amount in the app's settings. NFC is used only to read tags you register for clocking. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN check; the app never sees your biometric data.

Backups

  • Backup file: if you choose "Save backup file" or set an automatic backup folder, the app writes a file to the location you pick (for example Google Drive). The file is not encrypted, so keep it private. Where it is stored is your choice and is covered by that service's own policy.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's shifts and pay settings in your encrypted Google account backup or when you move to a new phone. This is done by Android and Google, not by the developer. Subscription status, app lock and a running shift are not included.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. The developer sees no payment information; the app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active.

Sharing

If you export a PDF or CSV report and share it (for example by email), that is your action and the file goes to the app you select. Nothing is shared automatically.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Because the developer holds none of your data, you control it entirely. You can delete all data at any time in Account → "Delete all my data", or by uninstalling the app. Delete any backup files you saved yourself from the place you saved them.

Estimates, not payslips

All figures are estimates for personal use based on the rules you enter. They do not replace your official payslip and are not provided by HMRC or your employer. Always check final amounts with your employer's payroll.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: olsenapps@proton.me
